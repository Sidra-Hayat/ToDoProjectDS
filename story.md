2.	Project Story:
To-Do List Manager – DS Universe Story
	In the land of Taskonia, every task lived as a small entity waiting to be completed.
	Sidra the Linked List connected each task in order, ensuring no task was ever lost.
	Liba the Stack handled urgent tasks, making sure the newest task got attention first.
	Array Ahmed Khan kept the completed tasks neatly organized for easy review.
	HashMap Hadia could instantly find any task in the kingdom by its unique ID.
	Together, they helped users manage their tasks efficiently, from adding new tasks to marking them done.
	Users entered the kingdom through a friendly login portal, where their tasks awaited.
	From the main dashboard, tasks could be added, searched, updated, or deleted with magic buttons.
	The team worked together to ensure no task was forgotten, making Taskonia productive and fun.
	And thus, in the land of To-Do, every task found its place and every user found peace of mind
